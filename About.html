about

Eman Khalid is an Upstate New York based artist who aspires to work in graphic design. Eman is currently studying digital media at Hudson Valley Community College where she is refining her skills in photography and photoshop. Eman is interested in using both traditional and digital art process and combining in her personal art. Eman creates digital illustrations using tools like Photoshop and Illustrator and she likes to experiment with various materials and textures to add on to her works and incorporate it in her digital art works. Outside of art she is interested in gardening and birdwatching. Spending time outside allows her to take break and inspire her for future works by observing patterns. In the future, Eman wants to create illustrations that combines nature with her work. She is interested in incorporating the motifs in her work. She hopes to continue to develop her digital skills.
